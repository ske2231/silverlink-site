Text Message Opt-In — How It Works

SilverLink LLC (operating 3J Mobile Auto Repair) · Public documentation of our SMS opt-in process

Where the call-to-action is displayed

Our business phone number, (405) 221-8900, is displayed as the call-to-action on the 3J Mobile Auto Repair website (https://ske2231.github.io/3j-mobile-auto-repair/), on our Google Business Profile, and in our advertising: "Call (405) 221-8900" to request a repair estimate or book service, 24/7.

Opt-in method 1: Verbal consent on a phone call

When a consumer calls (405) 221-8900 and requests a repair estimate, our agent tells the caller during the call, before any text message is sent:

"We can send your estimate by text message to the number you're calling from. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Is it OK to text your estimate to this number?"

The caller must verbally agree on the call before any message is sent. The first message sent is the caller's estimate; it identifies 3J Mobile Auto Repair and includes opt-out instructions. Callers who decline receive their estimate by email or verbally instead, and no text messages are sent.

Opt-in method 2: Text keyword

A consumer may also opt in by texting START, YES, or UNSTOP to (405) 221-8900. They receive a confirmation message confirming enrollment, describing the program (repair estimates and appointment updates), and stating that message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and that they can reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

Program disclosures

Messages are limited to repair estimates and appointment-related updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time; reply HELP for help, or call (405) 221-8900.
